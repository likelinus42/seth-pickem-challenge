      How It Works

      New to Pick'em or just need a refresher? Here's how to get started in three simple steps. Join, play, win.

      Join a Pool

      Choose between Otter Club ($20, straight-up winners) and Shark Club ($50, against the spread), or join both for more action. After signing up, you'll receive an email with your Sleeper invite link and Venmo details. Pay your entry fee via Venmo before kickoff of the first regular season game to secure your spot!

      Make Your Picks Each Week

      Log in to Sleeper and open your pool to make picks before each game's kickoff, with Thursday games locking first. In Otter Club, you'll pick the outright winner, while in Shark Club you'll pick against the spread. For the best experience, use the Sleeper app, available on both Apple and Android devices.

      Win Weekly & Season Prizes

      Once your picks are locked in, you can sit back, relax, and enjoy the games! Sleeper updates in real time with the winning teams and automatically tracks your score. Each week, the top three finishers are announced, and the overall leaderboard shows how you stack up across the entire season.
