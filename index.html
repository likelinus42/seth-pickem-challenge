Questions

FAQ

Can I join both pools?

Yes. Join Otter Club, Shark Club, or both. Playing both doubles your chances at weekly and season-long prizes.

How are payouts split?

Each pool's entry fees are split 20% for weekly winners and 80% for season-long standings. Payout amounts grow as more members join, see the calculator above.

Do I need a Sleeper account?

Yes. Sleeper hosts both pools, manages picks, and tracks standings. If you don't have an account, sign up free at sleeper.com.

How does scoring work?

Otter Club (straight-up): pick the outright winner of each game for one point per correct pick. Shark Club (against the spread): a point per pick that covers the spread, based on Sleeper's lines.

What happens with ties?

Weekly ties use Sleeper's tiebreaker: closest guess to the total points scored in the Monday night game, over or under. Still tied after that, overall point total decides it.

When do I get paid?

Weekly winners are paid after that week's games conclude. Season-long payouts go out after the final regular season game.
