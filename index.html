How It Works

New to Pick’em or just need a refresher? Here’s how to get started in three simple steps. Join, play, win.

Join a Pool
Choose between Otter Club ($20, straight-up winners) and Shark Club ($50, against the spread), or join both for more action. After signing up, you’ll receive an email with your Sleeper invite link and Venmo details. Pay your entry fee via Venmo before kickoff of the first regular season game to secure your spot!
Make Your Picks Each Week
Log in to Sleeper and open your pool to make picks before each game’s kickoff, with Thursday games locking first. In Otter Club, you’ll pick the outright winner, while in Shark Club you’ll pick against the spread. For the best experience, use the Sleeper app, available on both Apple and Android devices.
Win Weekly & Season Prizes
Once your picks are locked in, you can sit back, relax, and enjoy the games! Sleeper updates in real time with the winning teams and automatically tracks your score. Each week, the top three finishers are announced, and the overall leaderboard shows how you stack up across the entire season.

Ready to Join?

Lock in your spot for the season. Fill out the form below to sign up and get your invite to the Otter Club, Shark Club, or both.

FAQ

Got questions? We’ve got answers. Everything you need to know about how to sign up and play Pick’em Challenge.

Can I play in both pools?
Yes! You can join either Otter Club (Straight picks), Shark Club (Spread picks), or both. Playing in both doubles your chances to win weekly and season-long prizes.
How do payouts work?
Each pool’s total entry fees are split 20% for weekly winners and 80% for season-long standings. Payout amounts increase as more members join.
Do I need a Sleeper account to play?
Yes. Sleeper is the platform we use to host both pools, manage picks, and track standings. If you don’t have an account, you can sign up for free at sleeper.com.
How do I make picks?
Log in to Sleeper and open the pool you’ve joined.
  • In Otter Club (Straight), pick the outright winner of each game. You earn a point for every correct prediction.
  • In Shark Club (Spread), pick against the listed point spread. You earn a point if your pick “covers” the spread based on Sleeper’s game lines.
You can change any pick up until that game’s scheduled kickoff — Thursday Night Football locks first each week.
How are ties decided?
Weekly ties use Sleeper’s tiebreaker: guessing the total points scored in the Monday Night game. Whoever is closest to the actual total wins, regardless of whether the guess is over or under. In the event there is still a tie, Sleeper will use the overall point total to determine the winner.
When will winners be paid?
Weekly winners are paid after all games for that week have concluded. Season-long payouts are made after the final regular season game.
